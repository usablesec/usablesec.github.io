Usable Security and Privacy (USEC)

USEC events bring together security and usability experts from around the world to discuss recent advances and new perspectives on research in human factors in security and privacy. Workshops bring together researchers from different areas of computer science such as security, cryptography, visualization, artificial intelligence and machine learning as well as researchers from other domains such as psychology, social science, and economics.

USEC 2027 will be a workshop co-located with the IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy 2027.
